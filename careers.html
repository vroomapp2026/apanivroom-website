Customer Support Associate

Lusaka, Zambia (in-office) · Full-time (shift-based) · Reports to CEO
Details Apply

You'll be the first person a rider or driver talks to when something goes wrong — cancelled rides, payments, lost items, or safety concerns — and you'll make sure every one of them leaves the conversation looked after.

Key Responsibilities

  • Respond to rider and driver questions and issues by phone, in-app chat, and WhatsApp Business
  • Triage and escalate safety incidents according to Vroom's procedures
  • Resolve fare, payment, and wallet disputes, and help riders recover lost items
  • Support riders and drivers across all ride types — My-City, City-to-City, and Truck Jobs
  • Log recurring issues and flag product gaps to the team
  • Cover assigned shifts within the 6am–10pm coverage window

Compensation

  • Competitive, market-rate monthly base salary
  • Up to 100% of annual base salary in performance bonus, based on meeting service-quality and response-time targets

Minimum Qualifications & Skills

  • Fluent in English and 3 or more Zambian languages
  • Prior customer service or call-centre experience preferred
  • Calm, clear, and empathetic communicator under pressure
  • Comfortable with WhatsApp Business, admin dashboards, and basic spreadsheets
  • Grade 12 certificate required; diploma in a related field a plus

  • Email your CV and cover letter to careers@apanivroom.com with the position title as the subject

Field Service Associate

Kitwe or Lusaka, Zambia (field-based) · Full-time · Reports to Field Service Manager
Details Apply

You'll recruit Vroom's first riders and drivers in person and help them understand how Vroom works — a field role built on real conversations, not a desk job.

Key Responsibilities

  • Recruit both riders (passengers) and drivers in the field
  • Explain Vroom's ride types — My-City, City-to-City, and Truck Jobs, both shared and non-shared
  • Walk drivers through requirements, earnings, and the in-app approval process
  • Explain rewards, loyalty points, and referral incentives to riders
  • Track the rider and driver recruitment pipeline and report weekly progress
  • Run rider and driver referral campaigns
  • Represent Vroom at community meetups, bus stations, markets, and events

Compensation

  • Competitive, market-rate monthly base salary
  • Up to 200% of annual base salary in performance bonus, based on meeting recruitment and activation targets

Minimum Qualifications & Skills

  • Based in Kitwe or Lusaka and comfortable with regular field work
  • Prior sales, field marketing, or outreach experience preferred
  • Strong interpersonal skills; comfortable approaching strangers
  • Existing relationships with local drivers or transport operators a strong plus
  • Associate's degree (diploma) in Business, Sales, or Marketing required
  • Basic smartphone literacy
  • Fluent in at least two Zambian languages

Pricing Analyst

Lusaka, Zambia (in-office / hybrid) · Full-time · Reports to CEO
Details Apply

You'll own how Vroom prices every service line — My-City rides, City-to-City rides, and Truck Jobs — keeping fares and commissions competitive, profitable, and easy to understand.

Key Responsibilities

  • Monitor and analyze fare and commission structures across all ride and truck tiers
  • Benchmark Vroom's pricing against local competitors and recommend adjustments
  • Model the revenue and margin impact of proposed fare, commission, or promotional changes
  • Recommend and help implement fare, surge, and promotional pricing updates
  • Track demand, utilization, and price sensitivity by ride type and region
  • Partner with Finance on the pricing assumptions in Vroom's financial model
  • Own pricing for new service lines as they launch

Compensation

  • Competitive, market-rate monthly base salary
  • Up to 100% of annual base salary in performance bonus, based on meeting pricing and margin targets

Minimum Qualifications & Skills

  • Bachelor's degree in Economics, Finance, Business, Statistics, or a related field required
  • Strong Excel / spreadsheet modeling skills
  • Analytical mindset with strong attention to detail
  • Prior experience in pricing, revenue management, or financial analysis a strong plus
  • Comfortable turning large datasets into clear recommendations
  • Fluent in English; additional Zambian languages a plus

Details Apply
